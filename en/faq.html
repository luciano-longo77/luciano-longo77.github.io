Frequently asked questions

At a glance

Who is Luciano Longo?

An independent researcher (PhD, University of Palermo) in digital philology, textual criticism and TEI/XML scholarly digital editing. Alongside his research he works as District Leader - Service Manager in healthcare digital processes at Dedalus S.p.A.

What does his research focus on?

Authorial and genetic criticism, TEI/XML modelling and digital critical editions, with AI-assisted philological workflows under human oversight. Main projects: the digital critical edition of Il Castello dell’anima, a TEI + AI model of intertextuality, and the genetic edition of Attilio Bertolucci’s unpublished poems.

Where can I find his work and data?

In the GitHub repositories and in the open-access digital critical edition, archived on Zenodo with a DOI. His reference profiles for publications and identity are ORCID and HAL.

How to cite or contact him?

ORCID 0009-0005-7557-7546; e-mail luciano.longo@dedalus.com. The digital critical edition of Il Castello dell’anima is citable via the Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22058562.
